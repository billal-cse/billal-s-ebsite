Energy Saving Tips

💡 Turn Off Lights

Switch off lights when leaving a room.

❄️ Use AC Wisely

Set a reasonable temperature and clean filters regularly.

🔌 Avoid Standby Power

Unplug devices that are not being used.

🌞 Use Daylight

Use natural sunlight during daytime.
